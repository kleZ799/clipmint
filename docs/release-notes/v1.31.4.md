Every complaint from the feedback form that pointed at something fixable.

## "I asked for clips longer than a minute and got shorter ones"

**More than a minute** was read as *about* a minute, so clips between 50 and 70
seconds were allowed. Lengths written with a number, like **more than 1
minute**, **2 minutes**, **1.5 minutes** or **minimum 1 minute**, weren't read
at all.

Now:

- **More than a minute**, **over 1 min**, **at least 60 seconds**, **1 minute+**
  and **60s or more** are minimums. Every clip comes out at least that long.
- **Under 45 seconds**, **up to a minute** and **30s or less** are maximums.
- **2 minutes**, **1.5 minutes**, **half a minute** and **a minute and a half**
  are all understood, up to 4 minutes.
- **Cutting pauses never takes a clip under the length you asked for.** Before,
  a clip could be the right length and then lose a few seconds of pauses.
- A clip whose payoff is already near the start no longer comes out about 2.5
  seconds short.

## Clips that ended mid-word

The end of a clip was moved slightly to land on a quiet moment, and that move
could go either way. Sometimes it moved back into the last word, or forward into
the next person's first word. Now the end only moves later, never into anyone
speaking, and the start only moves earlier. When no sentence ends at the length
you asked for, the clip ends on the quietest moment in the last few seconds
instead of on the exact second.

## Arabic captions read backwards

Arabic and Hebrew captions had their words in left-to-right order. They now read
right to left, with the first word on the right. A name or number inside the
line, like "GTA 6", keeps its own order.

## "It clips the same part at the beginning"

The cold open replays a clip's loudest moment before the clip starts. When that
moment was only 3 to 6 seconds in, you'd see it again almost straight away,
which looked like the clip repeating itself. Now the replay only goes on when
the moment is at least 6 seconds in.

## A clearer error when Claude can't be reached

"Could not reach Anthropic" now says whether the request timed out or what got
in the way, like a proxy, a firewall or a certificate check.
