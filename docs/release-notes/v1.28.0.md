## Send feedback

Over 2,600 people have downloaded ClipMint, and almost none have said how it
went, because until now the only way was a GitHub issue. Now:

- **Send feedback** in the sidebar and on the launch card opens a small card
  with two ways in: a **quick form** that needs no account, or **GitHub**, with
  templates for a review, a bug or an idea.
- **Five stars.** Tap one and the form opens in your browser with that rating
  and your OS already filled in. Add a line if you like, then press Submit.
- **It asks after a run.** Close the player after your first run that makes
  clips and the card comes up. If you say **Not now**, it asks again after
  your 4th and 10th runs. Pick any way to answer and it never asks again.
- **A strip above Your clips**, "How did these come out?", stays until you
  answer. The x hides it for a week.

Good or bad, I read all of it, and it decides what gets built next.

The app sends nothing itself: everything opens in your browser, and nothing
leaves until you press Submit. The privacy policy has a new paragraph saying
exactly that.

---
**ClipMint.exe**: about 224 MB, Windows, self-contained. Unsigned, so
SmartScreen will warn: **More info → Run anyway**.

**ClipMint-linux-x86_64**: about 280 MB, x86-64, self-contained, glibc 2.35 or
newer. `chmod +x` it and run it. **Still untested on a desktop distribution.**
Every published build is started by the release workflow and asked for its
interface before the release exists, but nobody has yet made a Short with it
on Fedora or Ubuntu. Take `ClipMint-linux-README.txt` too.

**ClipMint-macOS-arm64.zip**: about 167 MB, Apple Silicon only. **Still a beta,
and still has never been run on a Mac.** I don't own one, so it is built and
checked by GitHub's macOS runners and published without a person opening it.
Uploading to YouTube has only been tested on Windows. If it doesn't start, or
signing in doesn't behave as described, that is a bug here:
`~/Movies/ClipMint/app.log` and
[an issue](https://github.com/kleZ799/clipmint/issues/new/choose) are how it gets
fixed.

Also published, for installs from before the rename only: `StreamToShorts.exe`,
`StreamToShorts-linux-x86_64` and `StreamToShorts-macOS-arm64.zip`, the same
three files under their old names. A fresh download wants the ClipMint ones.

On v1.5.0 or later on Windows, or on any Linux build? The app will offer this
to you.
