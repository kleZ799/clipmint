A fix for an error message that pointed at the wrong problem.

## "OpenAI refused the API key", when the key was fine

If Groq or OpenAI answered **403 Forbidden**, ClipMint said the key was refused
and told you to paste it again. That was wrong. A 403 means the provider read
your key and still turned the request down, usually because:

- **It doesn't serve your country or region.** Groq and OpenAI both block some
  places.
- **It's blocking your network.** A VPN, or a school or office network, can do
  this.
- **Your key can't use the model you picked.**

Pasting the key again never fixed any of these. The error now says which one it
was, quotes what the provider said, and tells you what to change: another
model, another network, or a provider that works in more places, like
**OpenRouter** (free models) or **Ollama** (runs on your own computer, no key).

A bad key still gets the old message. It only shows up when the key really is
wrong.
