Turn long videos into Shorts, on your own PC

ClipMint is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Give it a stream VOD, podcast, vlog or tutorial. It finds the best moments, cuts them into vertical Shorts, writes the titles and tags, and can upload them to your YouTube channel.

What it does

Finds the moments

Transcribes the video on your computer and ranks the moments most worth posting.

Cuts them vertical

Renders 9:16 clips with faces and gameplay framed the way Shorts need them.

Writes the packaging

Drafts titles, descriptions and tags for YouTube, plus captions for Reels and TikTok. You can edit every word.

Posts to your channel

Optionally uploads a clip to YouTube, now or on a schedule, when you press Upload.

Uploading to YouTube

ClipMint uses YouTube API Services to upload clips to your channel. It's optional, and it only happens when you press Upload to YouTube on a clip.

  • You sign in on Google's own page. ClipMint never sees your password.
  • It asks for two permissions: upload videos, and view your YouTube account so it can show which channel is connected and check an upload's privacy status. It can't delete or change anything on your channel.
  • Each upload is one clip, with the title, description, tags and schedule you see and can edit first. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • Your sign-in and upload records stay only on your computer. ClipMint has no servers and no accounts.
  • Disconnect in Settings gives the permission back and deletes what ClipMint stored. You can also remove access at Google's permissions page.

By connecting, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. How ClipMint handles your data is in the ClipMint privacy policy.
