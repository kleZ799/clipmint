    How it works

    1. Give it something long. Drop a file, or paste a YouTube link in the search bar.
    2. Say what you want. “Webcam top, 5 clips”. Or an exact span like “cut 14:45 to 15:30”.
    3. It watches the whole thing. Transcribes it, ranks the moments, and cuts vertical clips.
    4. Fix anything. Every clip opens in a player where you can move the in and out points, mute it, save it or delete it.

    Is this safe? Why Windows warned you

    Windows showed “Windows protected your PC” because this app is not code-signed. A signing certificate costs a few hundred dollars a year, and this is free software given away by one person. SmartScreen is reporting that, not that it found anything wrong — it says the same about most independent software on its first release.

    What you can check for yourself:

    • Every line of it is public. The source is on GitHub and the exe is built from it by GitHub’s own servers, in the open, with a log anyone can read.
    • The download is checksummed. Each release publishes a SHA-256 for the exe, and the app verifies it when updating itself.
    • It works with the network off. Transcribing and ranking run on this PC. It talks to YouTube to fetch a video, to your AI provider to rank moments, and to GitHub to check for updates. A clip goes to your YouTube channel only when you connect it and press Upload. Nothing else.

    The black windows that used to flash open during a run were ffmpeg, the video tool doing the cutting. They are hidden now — that was ours, and it was alarming for no reason.

    YouTube sign-in

    YouTube sometimes stops a download and asks it to prove it isn't a bot. The app already tries several ways round that on its own. This is what it falls back on when none of them work.

    Post to your channel

    Uploads a clip with its title, description and tags, now or on a schedule. You sign in on Google's own page, so the app never sees your password, and it can upload videos but not delete or change anything.

    Use your own Google client

    Uploading uses YouTube API Services. By connecting you agree to YouTube's Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies. Only upload videos you have the rights to.

    Free up space

    Deletes the big source videos the app downloaded, and any half-finished downloads. Your clips and transcripts stay. Re-running a video downloads it again.
